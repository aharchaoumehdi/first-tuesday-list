How we estimate

Value is the 2026 Harris County Appraisal District market value.

Original loan and the deed of trust date come from the foreclosure notice and county records.

Estimated balance assumes the loan was paid as scheduled since then, at the average 30-year mortgage rate of its year (Freddie Mac survey). Estimated equity is value minus that balance.

Estimated monthly rent is HUD's Small Area Fair Market Rent (FY2027) for the ZIP code and bedroom count: a typical rent for a modest home there, utilities included, not a quote. Homes, condos and townhomes only, when the bedroom count is known. Gross rent yield is a year of that rent divided by the value, before taxes, insurance, repairs and vacancy.

Not included: second liens, unpaid property taxes, HOA dues, missed payments and fees. At a trustee sale the opening bid can be close to the full debt: call the trustee before the sale. Compiled from public records. Not verified. Do your own title and lien research before bidding.
