Cypress, TX foreclosure auctions: Nov 3, 2026

41 of the 795 Harris County properties going to the foreclosure auction on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 are in Cypress, the 5th most of any city, spread over two ZIP codes. 77433 has the most (30), followed by 77429 (11). All 41 are single-family homes. One is an HOA lien sale (a homeowners association foreclosing for unpaid assessments); the other 40 are mortgage foreclosures. Four (10%) sit in a FEMA special flood hazard area (zone AE), above the 9% share countywide. The notices were filed between Sep 30 and Oct 13, seven of them in the last week before the filing deadline (since Oct 7). Four were posted more than once for this sale. 39 are set to sell in the 10 AM – 1 PM window; one at 11 AM – 2 PM and one at 12 PM – 3 PM.
