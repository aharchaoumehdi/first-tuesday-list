Baytown, TX foreclosure auctions: Nov 3, 2026

21 of the 795 Harris County properties going to the foreclosure auction on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 are in Baytown, spread over two ZIP codes. 77521 has the most (15), followed by 77520 (6). 20 are single-family homes and one is a commercial property.
