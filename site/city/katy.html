Katy, TX foreclosure auctions: Nov 3, 2026

56 of the 795 Harris County properties going to the foreclosure auction on Tuesday, November 3, 2026 are in Katy, the 4th most of any city, spread over three ZIP codes. 77449 has the most (38), followed by 77493 (15) and 77450 (3). 54 are single-family homes and two are commercial properties. Five are HOA lien sales (a homeowners association foreclosing for unpaid assessments); the other 51 are mortgage foreclosures. One (2%) sits in a FEMA special flood hazard area (zone AE), below the 9% share countywide. The notices were filed between Sep 30 and Oct 13, 16 of them in the last week before the filing deadline (since Oct 7). One was posted more than once for this sale. 52 are set to sell in the 10 AM – 1 PM window; two at 11 AM – 2 PM, one at 1 PM – 4 PM and one at 12 PM – 3 PM.
