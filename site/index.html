Questions

Before you sign up

Is it really free?

Yes. The map, every property page, the full spreadsheet of each sale and the weekly First Tuesday Brief are free, with no card. The optional membership adds the numbers: value, loan, estimated balance and equity, rent estimates and the lender.

Where do you get the data?

Public records. Foreclosure notices come from the Harris County Clerk, property records and aerial photos from the Harris County Appraisal District (HCAD), flood zones from FEMA and tax statements from Harris County Tax. AI-driven research reads every notice, and each property page links the original notice PDF.

How often is it updated?

We check the county every day. Notices for a sale can be filed until 21 days before it: for the Nov 3 sale that was Oct 13, so this list has its final count. The Brief every Wednesday says what's new, postponed and cancelled.

What's the catch?

None. We earn from the optional membership, $49 a month, or $39 for the first 100 founding members. We never sell your email or pass your inquiries to agents or lead buyers, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

Do you run the auction?

No. Sales are held by each notice's substitute trustee at the county's sale location. We're not the auctioneer, a broker or a law firm, and we don't give advice. Compiled from public records. Not verified. Do your own title and lien research before bidding.

Which counties do you cover?

Harris County today. Tell us which county you want next: leads@firsttuesdaylist.com.
